# Privacy Policy — Value Like Buffett – NSE Stock Screener

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Value Like Buffett – NSE Stock Screener ("the app") is a free desktop app published by Jude Finney (buildsbeyondAI).
This policy covers the Microsoft Store app and the self-hosted copy published from this repository.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
The developer receives no data about you or how you use the app.

## What stays on your PC

- The screener database (company filings, prices, your scans, thresholds and saved presets).
- A local log file used only for troubleshooting.

In the Store app these live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ValueLikeBuffett`. They never leave your PC, and uninstalling the app
or deleting that folder removes them.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only when you press **Refresh**, and then only to the public website of the National
Stock Exchange of India (nseindia.com) to download published prices and company filings. Those requests carry no
personal information. The app identifies itself honestly as "ValueLikeBuffett" in its user agent. NSE's own privacy
policy applies to its website.

The app window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which is part of Windows and is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
